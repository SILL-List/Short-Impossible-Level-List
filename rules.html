Level Requirements
01 — TIMING
Completion Time
The level must be completed (100%, or reach the end) in under 30 seconds. Measured from the start of gameplay to the moment of completion — decoration-only intros or outros before gameplay begins don't count against the clock, but no gameplay may extend past 30 seconds.
02 — INPUT DENSITY
CPS Limit
The level must not exceed 17 CPS (clicks per second) at any point, including short bursts. Levels requiring sustained or peak input above this ceiling will not be accepted, regardless of how good they look.
03 — MEMORY
Memory Cap
Total cumulative memory (blind sections requiring prior knowledge) across the whole level cannot exceed 15 seconds. This is a running total, not a single continuous block — multiple short memory sections still count toward the cap.
04 — LEGITIMACY
Bottable
The level must be physically possible to complete via legitimate input (keyboard, controller, or an accurate bot/macro replicating human-possible input). No glitched hitboxes, no patched mechanics, no TAS-only frame-perfect sequences no bot or human could realistically replicate.
05 — PRESENTATION
Quality Bar
The level must look and feel like something that could be rated by RobTop at a Featured or higher standard. Bare, unfinished decoration, placeholder blocks, or effort-less visual design will not be accepted, no matter how good the gameplay is.
Record Requirements
  • Raw, unedited footage of the full completion — no cuts, no speed changes.
  • Click/tap sounds audible throughout the entire clip.
  • Full run shown from start — no cutting straight to the final few seconds.
  • Cheat indicator enabled and visible for the entire run (e.g. Mega Hack's cheat indicator). Flags noclip, speedhack, click-between-frames, or other assisted input in real time.
  • No speedhacks, no macros/bots — unless the level is specifically bot-verified and marked as such.
  • Progress bar or percentage visible if the game mode shows one.
  • Player name and device (PC/mobile) included in the submission.
Note: Runs without a visible cheat indicator will not be accepted as verified records — this isn't optional, since it's the main way placement can trust reported difficulty and legitimacy without reviewing raw input files. Submissions with unclear audio or suspicious cuts will be rejected and may need to be resubmitted.
How to Submit

Level Submission

  • Post in #level-submissions
  • Level name & level ID
  • Creator name(s)
  • Gameplay video (raw footage preferred)
  • Approx. CPS & memory breakdown if known

Record Submission

  • Post in #record-submissions
  • Level name
  • Your username
  • Device used (PC/mobile)
  • Raw footage link
Conduct
  • Be respectful in submissions and discussion — no harassment of creators, verifiers, or placement decisions.
  • Difficulty disagreements are fine to discuss, but list placement is final unless new evidence changes the judgment.
  • Cheated, faked, or edited submissions will result in a ban.

Rules may be updated as the list grows. Check Discord pins for the most current version.
